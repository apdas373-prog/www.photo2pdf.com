Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion happens inside your browser using JavaScript.

Which image formats are supported?

Common browser-supported formats such as JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP can be used.

Can I change the image order?

Yes. Drag an image card to change its position before creating the PDF.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on modern mobile browsers.

Privacy

Image2PDF processes uploaded images locally in your browser. The images are not intentionally sent to a backend server for PDF conversion. PDF preferences are stored locally in your browser.
